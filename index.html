Why trust these numbers

There are no market-rate statistics on this site, and that is deliberate. Creator and UGC pricing varies by niche, audience country, engagement, deliverable and the brand's budget, and the "average rates" circulated online are mostly recycled between calculator pages with no dated survey behind them. Quoting them would give you a number that looks authoritative and means nothing.

So every rate input here is labelled "your assumption" and starts from an illustrative placeholder you are expected to overwrite. What the calculators contribute is the arithmetic: views × CPM ÷ 1,000, multiplied out across deliverables, usage rights and exclusivity, with each line itemised so you can see where the total came from. That arithmetic is covered by automated tests against worked examples before any change is published. Outputs are estimates built from your inputs, not financial advice. If something is wrong, the contact page reaches us.
